# Repository working instructions

- At the start of every session, read `LEARNINGS_CODEX.md` in full and apply relevant decisions, findings, and handoff notes before changing files.
- At the end of a session, append a concise dated entry to `LEARNINGS_CODEX.md` when the session produced useful findings, decisions, or follow-up work. Preserve earlier entries; do not rewrite the history.
- Never record credentials, access tokens, or other secrets in repository documentation or session notes.
- Keep product decisions and unresolved questions reflected in `docs/basic-design.md`.
